# DH Mass Talons

Demon Hunter into mass Druids of the Talon with Cyclone spam. Exploits Orc's limited dispel options. Cyclone removes key units from fights, Faerie Fire reduces armor.

- **Race:** Night Elf
- **Matchups:** EvO
- **Level:** Pro meta
- **Difficulty:** hard
- **Hero opener:** Demon Hunter

## How it plays

- DH first for Manaburn — drains Blademaster's mana, preventing Wind Walk escapes
- Druids of the Talon cast Cyclone — removes Grunts and key units from fights for 6 seconds
- Mass Cyclone means Orc can never fight at full strength — always 2-3 units disabled
- Faerie Fire reduces armor and reveals invisible units — BM can't hide
- Beastmaster from tavern for Quilbeast summons and Stampede ultimate
- Orc Spirit Walkers can Ethereal Form to avoid Cyclone — but NE can just focus other units
- Wins against: Orc melee compositions without heavy dispel
- Loses to: FS Headhunter builds that outrange Talons, mass Spirit Walkers

## Tier progression

- **Tier 1**
  - Buildings: Altar of Elders, Ancient of War, Moon Well, Hunter's Hall
  - Units: Archer
  - Goal: DH + Archers, creep to DH level 3 for Evasion
- **Tier 2**
  - Buildings: Tree of Ages, Ancient of Wind
  - Units: Druid of the Talon
  - Goal: Ancient of Wind for Druid of the Talon production, start massing Talons
- **Tier 3**
  - Buildings: Tree of Eternity, Ancient of Wind
  - Units: Druid of the Talon
  - Goal: Double Ancient of Wind, Master Talon upgrade for Storm Crow Form
  - Notes: Storm Crow Form lets Talons fly — attack and Cyclone from the air

## Hero skill order

- **Demon Hunter**: Mana Burn 3, Immolation 2, Evasion 1, Metamorphosis 1
- **Beastmaster**: Summon Bear 2, Summon Quilbeast 2, Summon Hawk 1

## Core upgrades

- Druid of the Talon Training
- Mark of the Talon
- nightelf backpack
