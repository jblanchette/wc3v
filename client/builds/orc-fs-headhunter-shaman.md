# FS Headhunter

Far Seer opener with Headhunters and Shamans. The most versatile Orc opener — Bloodlust makes Headhunters burst targets down, Hex and Purge shut down enemy heroes.

- **Race:** Orc
- **Matchups:** OvU, OvH
- **Level:** Ladder
- **Difficulty:** medium
- **Hero opener:** Far Seer

## How it plays

- Far Seer Spirit Wolves are free tanks that absorb Death Coil and Holy Light
- Headhunters with Berserker Upgrade have extreme DPS under Bloodlust
- Shaman Hex removes key enemy units from fights for several seconds
- Purge removes Unholy Aura speed, Brilliance Aura, Devotion Aura
- Tauren Chieftain second — Endurance Aura stacks with Bloodlust for insane attack speed
- Wins against: DK openers that rely on Death Coil micro, standard AM play
- Loses to: fast T2 pushes that arrive before Shamans get Bloodlust upgrade

## Tier progression

- **Tier 1**
  - Buildings: Altar of Storms, Orc Burrow, Barracks, Voodoo Lounge, War Mill
  - Units: Troll Headhunter/Berserker
  - Goal: FS + 2-3 Headhunters, creep with wolves tanking
- **Tier 2**
  - Buildings: Spirit Lodge
  - Units: Troll Headhunter/Berserker, Shaman
  - Goal: TC second hero, Shamans for Bloodlust + Purge, continue HH production
- **Tier 3**
  - Buildings: Fortress
  - Units: Troll Headhunter/Berserker, Shaman, Kodo Beast
  - Goal: Master Bloodlust upgrade, Kodo Beasts for Devour
  - Notes: T3 mainly for Shaman Master Training — the army composition stays HH/Shaman

## Hero skill order

- **Far Seer**: Chain Lightning 3, Feral Spirit 3, Earthquake 1
- **Tauren Chieftain**: Shockwave 2, Endurance Aura 2, War Stomp 1

## Core upgrades

- Shaman Training
- Berserker Upgrade
- orc backpack

## If you are new to this build

- Far Seer opens -- Chain Lightning bounces between enemies (great vs clumped units and casters), and Far Sight reveals a chunk of the map for free scouting. Creep with him toward level 3.
- Make Headhunters non-stop from the Barracks. They're ranged, so position them, let them shoot, and pull wounded ones back -- don't a-move them into melee.
- Get the Berserker upgrade at the War Mill (Headhunter -> Berserker: more HP and damage), and start Melee/Ranged Weapons + Unit Armor level 1 early -- cheap and huge for the whole army.
- Shaman at T2 cast Bloodlust on your Headhunters (big attack-speed boost). Don't worry about Wind Riders, Taurens, or T3 yet -- Headhunter + Shaman with Bloodlust is a complete army.

## Common mistakes

- **A-moving Headhunters into the enemy melee line.**
  - Fix: They're fragile ranged units. Stand them at max range, attack, and micro the front ones backward -- let your hero and any Grunts soak the hits.
- **Forgetting the Berserker upgrade and attack/defense at the War Mill.**
  - Fix: Berserker and Weapons/Armor level 1 are cheap and transformative. Queue them as soon as you can afford them.
- **Not casting Bloodlust in fights.**
  - Fix: Bloodlusted Headhunters attack ~40% faster. Cast it on your damage dealers at the start of every engagement -- unspent Shaman mana is damage you didn't do.
- **Throwing the Far Seer in alone or wasting Chain Lightning.**
  - Fix: Chain Lightning wants a clump (multiple enemy units close together) -- fire it then, not at a lone unit. Keep the FS behind the army; a dead hero is a lost game.

## Before you try it

- Comfortable positioning ranged units and pulling wounded ones back from the front.
- Know how to cast Chain Lightning into a clump and Bloodlust on your own damage dealers.
