# DK Destroyer

DK Fiend opener that priorities a fast T3 for Destroyer tech. Destroyers' Devour Magic hard-counters caster-heavy armies like NE Dryads or HU Priests.

- **Race:** Undead
- **Matchups:** UvN, UvH, UvO
- **Level:** Pro meta
- **Difficulty:** hard
- **Hero opener:** Death Knight

## How it plays

- Standard DK Ghoul T1 — creep efficiently, build 4-5 Ghouls before teching to Fiends at T2
- Lich second with Frost Nova for burst damage in engagements
- Rush T3 for Destroyers — they eat buffs/debuffs and counter caster compositions
- Destroyers absorb mana for healing — self-sustaining vs magic-heavy armies
- Dark Ranger third hero — Silence shuts down NE bears/dryads, Black Arrow raises skeletons
- Wins against: NE Dryad/Bear compositions, HU Priest/Breaker armies
- Loses to: fast timing pushes that hit before Destroyers are online

## Tier progression

- **Tier 1**
  - Buildings: Crypt, Graveyard, Altar of Darkness, Tomb of Relics
  - Units: Ghoul
  - Goal: DK + 4-5 Ghouls, aggressive creep
- **Tier 2**
  - Buildings: Slaughterhouse
  - Units: Crypt Fiend, Obsidian Statue
  - Goal: Lich second, Statues for sustain, begin T3 upgrade immediately
- **Tier 3**
  - Buildings: Black Citadel
  - Units: Destroyer, Obsidian Statue
  - Goal: Destroyers ASAP — Devour Magic is the win condition
  - Notes: Rushed Destroyers specifically to counter caster-heavy compositions

## Hero skill order

- **Death Knight**: Death Coil 3, Unholy Aura 3, Death Pact 1
- **Lich**: Frost Nova 3, Frost Armor 1, Dark Ritual 1
- **Dark Ranger**: Silence 2, Black Arrow 1

## Core upgrades

- Web
- Destroyer Form
- undead backpack
