# DH Standard

The standard Night Elf build. Demon Hunter first for Manaburn harassment, fast tech to T3 for Druid of the Claw (bears). Bears in Bear Form have massive HP and Roar aura.

- **Race:** Night Elf
- **Matchups:** EvU, EvO, EvH
- **Level:** Ladder
- **Difficulty:** medium
- **Hero opener:** Demon Hunter

## How it plays

- DH first — Manaburn drains enemy hero mana, shutting down their abilities
- Immolation provides passive AoE damage — good for fast creeping
- Fast tech through T2 to T3 — the goal is Bears as fast as possible
- Druids of the Claw in Bear Form have 960 HP and Roar gives 25% damage aura
- KotG second hero for Entangle + Treants — lockdown and free reinforcements
- Dryads provide Abolish Magic to remove debuffs and Slow Poison for kiting
- Wins against: most standard openers across all matchups
- Loses to: fast timing pushes that hit during the vulnerable T2 transition

## Tier progression

- **Tier 1**
  - Buildings: Altar of Elders, Ancient of War, Moon Well, Hunter's Hall
  - Units: Archer
  - Goal: DH + 3-4 Archers, creep aggressively with Immolation
- **Tier 2**
  - Buildings: Tree of Ages, Ancient of Lore
  - Units: Dryad
  - Goal: Tree of Ages, Ancient of Lore x2, Dryads while teching to T3
- **Tier 3**
  - Buildings: Tree of Eternity
  - Units: Druid of the Claw, Dryad
  - Goal: Master Bears — Bear Form with Roar and Rejuvenation
  - Notes: Bears are arguably the best T3 unit in the game — the entire build leads here

## Hero skill order

- **Demon Hunter**: Mana Burn 3, Immolation 2, Evasion 1, Metamorphosis 1
- **Keeper of the Grove**: Entangling Roots 2, Force of Nature 2, Thorns Aura 1

## Core upgrades

- Druid of the Claw Training
- Mark of the Claw
- nightelf backpack
