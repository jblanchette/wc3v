# AM Rifle

Archmage into Footmen transitioning to Riflemen and Sorceresses. Rifle range controls space, Slow from Sorceresses kites melee armies.

- **Race:** Human
- **Matchups:** HvN, HvH, HvO
- **Level:** New to WC3
- **Difficulty:** easy
- **Hero opener:** Archmage

## How it plays

- Archmage Water Elementals are free units that trade well early — micro them aggressively
- Brilliance Aura gives permanent mana regen to all nearby units — enables Blizzard spam
- Riflemen have the longest range of any T1 ground unit — kite melee effectively
- Mountain King second for Storm Bolt stun and Bash — shuts down enemy hero plays
- Priests with Inner Fire buff Riflemen damage, Heal sustains the army between fights
- Spell Breakers at T2 — Control Magic steals summons, Spell Immunity blocks nukes
- Wins against: most standard openers, NE Archer builds, Orc Grunt builds
- Loses to: mass Mountain Giants with Taunt, UD Destroyer transitions

## Tier progression

- **Tier 1**
  - Buildings: Altar of Kings, Barracks, Farm, Blacksmith
  - Units: Footman, Rifleman
  - Goal: AM + Footmen for creeping, transition to Rifles
- **Tier 2**
  - Buildings: Arcane Sanctum, Keep
  - Units: Rifleman, Priest, Spell Breaker
  - Goal: MK second hero, Arcane Sanctum for Priests + Spell Breakers
- **Tier 3**
  - Buildings: Castle
  - Units: Knight
  - Goal: Castle for Knight production and Master Training upgrades
  - Notes: T3 is optional — many games end at T2 with Rifle/Caster army

## Hero skill order

- **Archmage**: Blizzard 3, Summon Water Elemental 3, Brilliance Aura 1
- **Mountain King**: Storm Bolt 3, Thunder Clap 1, Bash 1
- **Paladin**: Holy Light 2, Devotion Aura 1

## Core upgrades

- Priest Training
- Defend
- human backpack

## If you are new to this build

- Peasants build on-site. Pull a peasant (or 2-3) off mining onto a building to finish it faster -- especially the Town Hall when rushing T2 -- then send them back to the mine.
- Summon Water Elementals with the Archmage at every fight and every creep camp. They're free, expendable units -- re-summon when they expire.
- Riflemen out-range everything on the ground at T1. Stand still, let them shoot, pull back wounded ones. Footmen tank; Rifles shoot -- don't a-move Rifles into melee.
- Get a Blacksmith early for attack/defense upgrades, and Priests (Inner Fire + Heal) once you're at T2. Don't worry about Knights or T3 for a long time -- Rifle + Priest wins most games on its own.

## Common mistakes

- **Letting the Archmage's mana sit unused.**
  - Fix: Brilliance Aura refills it constantly. Summon a Water Elemental, then Blizzard their army or workers -- unspent mana is damage you didn't do.
- **Riflemen melting in melee because you a-moved.**
  - Fix: Position them, shoot from max range, and micro the front-line ones backward. Let your Footmen and Water Elementals soak the hits.
- **Getting all-in'd early with no Militia call.**
  - Fix: Call to Arms turns your peasants into Militia for 45 seconds. Use it to survive an early rush, then send them back to mining.
- **Skipping Blacksmith upgrades.**
  - Fix: +1 attack on a Rifle line is enormous. Get Iron Forged Swords / Iron Plating level 1 going as soon as the Blacksmith is up.

## Before you try it

- Comfortable summoning and re-summoning Water Elementals during fights.
- Know how to Call to Arms (Militia) to defend an early push and how to send peasants back to work.
