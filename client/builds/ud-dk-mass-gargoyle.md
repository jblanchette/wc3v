# DK Gargoyle

DK opener into mass Ghouls for lumber, then flood Gargoyles from multiple Crypts. Air superiority overwhelms Orc ground armies. Dark Ranger or Dreadlord third hero for utility.

- **Race:** Undead
- **Matchups:** UvO
- **Level:** Pro meta
- **Difficulty:** hard
- **Hero opener:** Death Knight

## How it plays

- DK first with Death Coil — creep efficiently, mass Ghouls for lumber harvesting
- Delayed T2 (7:00-8:00) because heavy Ghoul investment feeds the Gargoyle transition
- Lich second with Frost Nova — burst damage on Orc ground clumps
- T3 for mass Gargoyle production from double Crypt — quantity is key
- Dark Ranger third hero for Silence (shuts down Shadow Hunter/TC) and Black Arrow skeletons
- Obsidian Statues keep Gargoyles alive through sustained healing
- Wins against: Orc ground-heavy builds (Grunts/Raiders) that lack anti-air
- Loses to: fast Wind Rider + Batrider transitions that win the air war

## Tier progression

- **Tier 1**
  - Buildings: Crypt, Altar of Darkness, Tomb of Relics, Ziggurat, Ziggurat
  - Units: Ghoul
  - Goal: DK + mass Ghouls for lumber, creep to level 3
- **Tier 2**
  - Buildings: Graveyard, Crypt, Slaughterhouse
  - Units: Ghoul, Gargoyle
  - Goal: Lich second, begin Gargoyle production, expand if possible
- **Tier 3**
  - Buildings: Black Citadel, Temple of the Damned
  - Units: Gargoyle, Obsidian Statue, Banshee
  - Goal: Mass Gargoyles + Statues, Dark Ranger third hero for Silence
  - Notes: Double Crypt Gargoyle production is critical for reaching critical mass

## Hero skill order

- **Death Knight**: Death Coil 3, Unholy Aura 3, Death Pact 1
- **Lich**: Frost Nova 3, Frost Armor 2, Dark Ritual 1

## Core upgrades

- undead backpack
