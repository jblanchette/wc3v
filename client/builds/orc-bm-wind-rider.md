# BM Wind Rider

Blademaster opener with heavy early harassment, transitioning into Wind Rider air superiority. Denies expansion and wins on economic disruption.

- **Race:** Orc
- **Matchups:** OvU
- **Level:** Pro meta
- **Difficulty:** hard
- **Hero opener:** Blademaster

## How it plays

- BM's Mirror Image and Wind Walk make him nearly impossible to catch early game
- Constant worker harassment forces UD to divert Acolytes and delays expansion
- Wind Riders fly over Undead army positioning and attack buildings directly
- Shadow Hunter second hero for Serpent Wards and Healing Wave sustain
- Wins against: UD expansions that come too early or are poorly defended
- Loses to: well-timed Lich Nova + DK coil that kills Wind Riders before critical mass

## Tier progression

- **Tier 1**
  - Buildings: Altar of Storms, Orc Burrow, Barracks, Voodoo Lounge
  - Units: Grunt
  - Goal: BM harass enemy base, Grunts for creeping
- **Tier 2**
  - Buildings: Beastiary
  - Units: Wind Rider
  - Goal: Shadow Hunter second, start Wind Rider production from Bestiary
- **Tier 3**
  - Buildings: Fortress
  - Units: Wind Rider
  - Goal: Mass Wind Riders with Envenomed Weapons upgrade
  - Notes: T3 mainly for unit upgrades — army is already Wind Riders at T2

## Hero skill order

- **Blademaster**: Wind Walk 3, Critical Strike 3, Mirror Image 1
- **Shadow Hunter**: Hex 2, Healing Wave 2, Serpent Ward 1

## Core upgrades

- Envenomed Spears
- orc backpack
