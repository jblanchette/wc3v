Where are my replay files?

Warcraft III saves your replays as .w3g files in the standard Documents folder.

Windows Documents\Warcraft III\Replays
Battle.net Documents\Warcraft III\BattleNet\<account>\Replays
W3Champions Documents\Warcraft III\Replays\W3Champions

Drag any .w3g file onto the Drop or upload a replay zone, or click it to open a file picker. The replay is parsed entirely on this machine; nothing leaves your browser.

What WC3V does

Pro build library Tournament builds by race and matchup, each with the pro replay behind it. Replay simulator Warcraft III can't rewind a replay. This rebuilds the game from the file, so you can jump to any second. Desktop companion Watches your replay folder and grades each game as you finish it.
WC3V

Free, open-source replay analysis for Warcraft III. Browse pro builds, drop a .w3g for a full breakdown that parses entirely in your browser, or compare your replay to a pro's for letter-graded feedback.
