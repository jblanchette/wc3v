Where are my replay files?

Warcraft III saves your replays as .w3g files in the standard Documents folder.

Windows Documents\Warcraft III\Replays
Battle.net Documents\Warcraft III\BattleNet\<account>\Replays
W3Champions Documents\Warcraft III\Replays\W3Champions

Drag any .w3g file onto the Upload a replay button — or click it to open a file picker. The replay is parsed entirely on this machine; nothing leaves your browser.
