Replay folders

WC3V reads the replays Warcraft III already saves. Your replays never leave this machine — parsing happens here, and nothing about your games is uploaded anywhere.

The app does fetch two things from wc3v.com's CDN: the icon art for build orders, and map data for a map it has not seen before. Neither carries anything about you or your games.

Parsing your history
